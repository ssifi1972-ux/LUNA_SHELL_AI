# LUNA_SHELL_AI – SIFI Lunar Grounding Challenge
NASA Lunar Surface Grounding Challenge – Submission by Sifi Boukhari, Batna, DZ

## Problem (Webinar 1)
- Suit charges by tribocharging + plasma
- No photoelectron emission in PSR shadows
- Astronaut = walking high-voltage capacitor
- Arc risk on contact with vehicle

## SIFI Solution – Gravity Eliminates EVA
- No human in PSR after 3-day setup
- 10x10m concave Mylar mirror on sunny rim (136kW) + 3 heliostats (6 mirrors)
- 10m quartz channel with internal 45° Mylar mirrors beams sunlight into PSR
- Inclined gravity railway 5-45°: 500kg icy regolith descends by lunar gravity (1.62 m/s²), empty ascends via counterbalanced cable pulley – 0kW transport, 44kW regenerative braking
- Electrostatic plate -10kV in PSR collects 10kg/h charged dust FREE using same high charge
- Rotary kiln quartz retort extracts water, thermosiphon condenses at cold apex
- Grounded 2m lunar grounding rod + steel cable = grounding

## Performance
- 10 tons/day water, 70% energy saving, 0 EVA risk
